IELTS READING · QUICK PRACTICE

Urban trees and cooler streets

In many cities, trees do more than improve the appearance of a street. Their shade can lower surface temperatures, while leaves provide habitats for birds and insects. However, urban planners must choose species carefully: trees that need too much water may not survive during long dry periods. Some city councils now involve local residents in caring for young trees, which can increase a sense of responsibility for shared public spaces.

1. What is one effect of shade from trees?
2. Why should planners select tree species carefully?
3. What can resident involvement encourage?
